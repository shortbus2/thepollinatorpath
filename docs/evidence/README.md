## Rules

- Evidence must identify exact sources, dates, commits or snapshot IDs, checksums, methods, policy IDs, and decision IDs.
- Significant historical decisions must be reproducible without relying on human memory.
- Generated evidence never replaces source data.
- Durable reconciliation decision evidence is stored under `docs/evidence/reconciliation/YYYY-MM/<decision-id>/`.
